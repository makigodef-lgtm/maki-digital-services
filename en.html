A useful lesson. No synthetic narrator.

01 / 04

Start with customer needs

A post matters commercially only when it answers a real need. Identify the customer before choosing Facebook, TikTok or WhatsApp.

02 / 04

Define your audience

Consider a tailor in Kinshasa. The audience might be people needing custom clothing for an upcoming occasion.

03 / 04

Make a clear offer

Show real work, state lead times, explain terms and make it easy to request a quote.

04 / 04

One hundred views do not mean one hundred customers. Track enquiries, quote requests and actual paid orders separately.
